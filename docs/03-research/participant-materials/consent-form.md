## What is this study about?

We are testing a software prototype that detects driving hazards in dashcam videos and issues alerts to the driver. We want to measure how quickly people react to these alerts and how easy the system is to use.

---

## What will I be asked to do?

You will:
1. Watch a series of short driving video clips (5–10 seconds each) on a computer screen.
2. Press the SPACEBAR whenever you perceive a hazard (e.g. pedestrian, sudden brake, cyclist).
3. Fill out 2–3 short questionnaires about workload and usability.

The whole session takes about **30 minutes**.

There is no driving simulator, no physical risk, no time pressure between trials.

---

## Will my data be confidential?

Yes. You will be assigned a participant ID (e.g. P07). Your name will not appear in any data file. The signed consent form is stored separately from the response data, in a secured folder, and is not linked to your responses.

Aggregated results (averages across all participants) may appear in:
- The researcher's Bachelor's thesis
- A potential conference or journal publication

No individual-level data will be published.

---

## Can I stop?

Yes. You may withdraw at any time, for any reason, without explanation, and your data will be deleted. There is no penalty for withdrawing.

---

## Are there any risks?

The study uses passive video viewing. Some participants may experience mild visual fatigue from screen viewing. We will offer breaks between conditions.
